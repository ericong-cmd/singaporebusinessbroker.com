## What a buyer is really underwriting

Two construction or M&E business businesses with the same profit rarely sell for the same money. The gap is confidence: how quickly a buyer can verify the earnings, and how much of the operation walks out of the door with you.

That is why preparation is worth more than negotiation. Twelve to eighteen months of work on the four value drivers above will usually move the price further than any amount of arguing at the term sheet stage.

## Where to start

Get an indicative range first, then decide whether to prepare or to go to market. The estimator takes about two minutes and will tell you which of those two conversations you should be having.

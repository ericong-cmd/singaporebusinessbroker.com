The owner had two credible internal successors and cared more about the two hundred staff than about maximising the headline number. A trade sale would likely have paid more and would have meant consolidation.

The deal was funded partly by a bank and partly by a vendor loan repaid over three years, secured against the shares. Progressive wage and CPF compliance was reviewed thoroughly before the bank would lend, which is normal in this sector and worth doing early.

The buyer needed a Singapore base with certifications already in place and MNC customers already approved. That is a capability that costs more to build than to buy, which is where the premium came from.

The work before going to market was almost entirely about customer approvals: confirming which contracts survived a change of control and which required re-qualification, so that the buyer could price certainty rather than discount for it.

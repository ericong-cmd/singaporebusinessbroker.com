Questions owners ask first

What does it cost to sell through you?

A modest engagement fee to prepare the materials, then a success fee as a percentage of the sale price, paid at completion. No sale, no success fee. We set out fees in writing before you commit.

Will my staff, customers or competitors find out?

Not from us. Buyers first see an anonymous teaser. Your company name is only released after a signed NDA and your approval, buyer by buyer.

Is my business too small for you?

We advise on businesses from about S$1m in value, with most work between S$3m and S$30m. If yours is smaller, the valuation tool still gives you a range and a preparation guide.

How long does a sale take?

Typically six to nine months from mandate to completion. Preparation before going to market is what most affects the timeline and the price.

Do I need to stay after the sale?

Usually a handover of 6 to 12 months. How long, and on what terms, is negotiated as part of the deal. Low owner dependency shortens it.
